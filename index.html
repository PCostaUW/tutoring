How it works

Every session is a worked problem

We follow the same method we'd use at the whiteboard: find where the gap really is, build a plan around it, then practice until it holds.

STEP 1

Diagnose

Your first session can be a diagnostic: we work a few problems together and map exactly where things break down — often earlier than you'd guess.

STEP 2

Plan

We build a session plan around your course, your timeline, and your goals — a test in two weeks looks different from a semester of support.

STEP 3

Practice

You do the work with a guide beside you. We explain, you solve, and we don't move on until you can do it without us.

FAQ

Common questions

Are sessions online or in person?
Sessions are online by default, over video call with a shared whiteboard. Ask about in-person options when you inquire.
How much does a session cost?
Rates depend on subject and level — mention what you need in the inquiry form and we'll send pricing before anything is booked. No charge until you've confirmed.
What happens in a diagnostic session?
We work a short set of problems together, chosen for your course. From how you approach them — not just whether you get them right — we map where the real gaps are and hand you a written plan. It's useful even if you decide not to continue with us.
Do you assign homework?
If it helps, yes — short, targeted practice between sessions. Your actual coursework always comes first; we won't pile on.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
Your booking confirmation email has reschedule and cancel links. We ask for 24 hours' notice when possible.
Can parents sit in?
Always welcome, especially for younger students or a first session.
